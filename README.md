
# Bluetooth Speaker – Electronics Engineering Project

## Project Overview

This project focuses on the design and development of a Bluetooth speaker as part of an electrical engineering degree at NTNU.

The goal is to develop a functional audio system by combining custom electronics, Bluetooth communication, audio amplification and mechanical design.

## Technologies and Tools

- **PCB Design:** KiCad
- **3D Modelling:** SolidWorks
- **Bluetooth Module:** Microchip BM83
- **Audio Amplifier:** Texas Instruments TPA3129D2
- **Simulation and Testing:** Oscilloscope, multimeter and laboratory equipment
- **Manufacturing:** 3D printing and PCB assembly

## Project Development

The project involves several engineering disciplines:

### Electronics
- PCB design and component selection
- Power supply design
- Bluetooth and audio integration
- Circuit testing and troubleshooting

### Mechanical Design
- Custom speaker enclosure developed in SolidWorks
- Design for 3D printing
- Integration of electronic components

### Testing and Troubleshooting
- Power supply measurements
- Bluetooth module configuration
- Audio signal testing
- Investigation of startup and voltage issues
